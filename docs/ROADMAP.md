# Near-term roadmap / 近期路线图

These are priorities, not released capabilities or delivery dates. PRs carry
their actual verification evidence; the changelog records delivered changes.

1. **Conversation quality:** bounded question closure, fewer table-level echoes,
   visible use of public evidence. Do not prescribe a winner or claim balance.
2. **Player-path verification:** real browser/backend continuation, campaign and
   failure recovery; expand platform coverage and measure branch coverage.
3. **Verified delivery:** build/test the exact archive and prepare draft releases,
   retaining explicit human publication approval and honest model-test limits.

近期只聚焦：对话质量、玩家完整路径验证、可验证发布。不同时堆叠新角色、新板子，
不把路线图当成已实现功能或交付日期。完成情况看 PR 与更新日志；真实模型证据单列。
